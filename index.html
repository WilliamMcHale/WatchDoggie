How it works, in three steps

Built around how a busy daycare floor actually runs.

1

Book with real places left

See every day's places left at a glance. A full day can't be overbooked: WatchDoggie offers the nearest day with room instead.

2

Vaccines checked at the door

Vaccines that expire within 14 days turn amber. Expired ones turn red and the dog can't be booked or checked in until the record is updated.

3

One tap per update

Staff tap Ate, Walked, Played or Napped. Each owner opens their own Pupdate with a personal code and sees only their own dog's day.
